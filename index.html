ZONAR Studios

A unisex fashion store with product sliders for menswear, womenswear and watches.

  • Hover a product photo to flip through its angles.
  • Add items to the cart and change the quantities.
  • Tap the heart to save a favourite.
  • Write a review and watch the star rating update.

Your cart, favourites and reviews are saved in your browser, so they are still there after a refresh. Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Aura Motion

A site for a branding and web agency, with home, services and portfolio pages.

Make your browser window narrow and the menu turns into a sidebar. Hover a portfolio piece to see its details. Built with HTML and CSS.
